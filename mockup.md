# Pet Care & Pet Shop Management System

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Name

Pet Care & Pet Shop Management System

### Project Description

A comprehensive multi-branch pet care and pet shop management platform that supports:

* Pet product sales
* Pet grooming and care services
* Service booking management
* Inventory and warehouse management
* Order and payment processing
* Supplier management
* Promotion and voucher management
* Customer and pet profile management
* AI-powered pet breed recognition and chatbot support
* Reporting and analytics

The system supports three main user groups:

1. Customers
2. Shop Staff
3. System Administrators

---

# 2. User Roles

## Customer

Can:

* Register/Login
* Manage profile
* Manage pet profiles
* Browse products
* Purchase products
* Book services
* Review products and services
* View orders and bookings
* Receive notifications

---

## Shop Staff

Can:

* Manage bookings
* Manage inventory
* Manage orders
* Manage room schedules
* Handle stock transfers
* View shop inventory
* Manage assigned tasks

---

## Administrator

Can:

* Manage users and roles
* Manage shops and branches
* Manage products and categories
* Manage services
* Manage inventory
* Manage suppliers
* Manage vouchers and promotions
* Access dashboards and reports
* Configure permissions
